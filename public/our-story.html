A Legacy Born in 1946

In 1946, a group of determined women in Cincinnati came together with a shared belief: that women who served our country deserved a place to belong, lead, and continue making a difference[cite: 2].

They founded Auxiliary Unit 1946 of The American Legion—a sisterhood built on service, patriotism, and strong values[cite: 2].

Today, we carry that legacy forward as American Legion Greater Cincinnati Women's Post 644[cite: 2].

We honor their courage by living their mission—supporting veterans, strengthening our community, and empowering women to lead[cite: 2].
