Welcome

★

Honoring veterans,
strengthening community,
empowering women.

Serving those who served, and building a community for women veterans.

Welcome to American Legion Greater Cincinnati Women's Post 644. We are a vibrant community of women who have worn the cloth of our nation, dedicated to fellowship, mutual aid, and community service.

Whether you are looking to connect with fellow veterans, continue serving your community, or find a place where your military experience is truly understood and valued, you belong here.

We invite you to explore our story, check out our upcoming events, and drop in to visit us at our next meeting.

We can't wait to welcome you.
