VISIT US

★

We can't wait to meet you.

Step into a room where your service is understood, your presence is valued, and you are always welcomed as family.

If you've been thinking about visiting, we're glad you're here.

Walking into a room where you don't know anyone can feel a little intimidating. We understand that because every one of us attended our first meeting once.

The good news is, you won't be walking into a room full of strangers for long.

From the moment you arrive, someone will greet you, introduce you to our members, and make sure you feel at home. There's nothing you need to do except walk through the door. We'll take it from there.

Some of our members wear American Legion attire. Others dress casually. Wear whatever makes you feel comfortable.

If bringing a friend or family member helps make that first visit a little easier, you're welcome to do that too.

There's no pressure to become a member. We simply want the opportunity to meet you and let you get to know us.

Whenever you're ready, we'll be here to welcome you.
