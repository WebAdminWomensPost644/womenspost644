Eligibility

★

Women veterans who served at least one day of federal active duty since December 7, 1941, and were honorably discharged—or are still serving honorably—may be eligible for membership.

View official American Legion eligibility guidance

Membership Benefits

★
  • A supportive sisterhood of women who understand your journey.
  • Opportunities to serve veterans, families, and our community.
  • Events, fellowship, and continued connection throughout the year.
