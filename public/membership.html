WHO WE ARE

★
👥

Ohio's only all-female American Legion Post. Founded in 1946 by women veterans, for women veterans. Our legacy continues today.

ELIGIBILITY

★

Anyone who has served at least one day of active duty since December 7, 1941 and was honorably discharged or is still serving honorably is eligible for membership.
