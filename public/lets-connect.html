A Personal Welcome ♡

Thank you for taking the time to visit Greater Cincinnati Women's Post 644.

If you're here because you're looking for connection, friendship, purpose, or simply a place where your military service is understood, I hope you'll find that you've come to the right place.

Our Post was founded in 1946 by women veterans who believed there should be a place where women who served could belong, support one another, and continue making a difference. Nearly eighty years later, that same spirit still guides us.

Whether you've recently left the military or your service was many years ago, you'll find women here who understand your journey because they've walked similar paths.

I hope you'll join us for a meeting. There is nothing you need to do except walk through the door—we'll take it from there.

On behalf of all of us at Greater Cincinnati Women's Post 644, welcome. We look forward to meeting you.

Melanie Veal

Commander
Greater Cincinnati Women's Post 644
